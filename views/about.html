About Us

Our story

TK Diamond Drilling is a 100% First Nation owned business Founded by a father son duo that has 35 combined years in the drilling industry. As a First Nation owned company, our core values include respect for Indigenous rights, a commitment to safety, and sustainable economic growth with the First Nations communities by working with them.

TK Diamond Drilling has the professional management skills with Kevin being an operation manager for 15 years at a reputable company. Tyrel's experience include drilling/foreman on projects across Canada since right out of high school.
